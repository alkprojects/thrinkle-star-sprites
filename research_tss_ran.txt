 Story 
Load Ran is an girl that uses her broom to fly and fight. She has a counterpart named Dark Ran and she plays the same as her and is only a palette change.

Weaknesses
 | 
 Surprise Factor : Her EX attacks can move extremely quickly, and can fly to unexpected places. This helps makes up for the fact that they're otherwise a quite predictable attack, and gives some chance to win the game abruptly without the other player using their bombs. 
 | 
 Worst Bomb : Ran has an extra animation when using her bomb, so she has less invul time to reposition after a bomb. 
 Bad Charge Attack : The charge attack does fairly low damage and has terrible horizontal coverage. In conjunction with her low movement speed, Ran tends to have a hard time controlling her screen. It's also unreliable at killing fireballs that are right in front of you, making it a bad panic button. Most low speed characters have impressive charge attacks to support them, but Ran lacks this and as a result has overall very bad defense. 
 Bad Boss : The boss's body slam is a respectable attack, but the other three attacks are slow, don't cover much space, and are easily ignored. 
 Inconsistent EX : Outsize of its potential for surprise attacks, the EX doesn't have much strength. They're very small and generally predictable, and only exist on screen for a very brief time. Because they're so reliant on speed and catching the other player off guard, they need to be part of an extremely long combo and be fired outside of slowdown to be particularly effective. If this is done, however, they make pretty good attacks.
